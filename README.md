## 🌱 My Story

Have you ever seen a tiny plant growing through a crack in a rock?

At first, the rock looks stronger, larger, and completely in the way.

But the plant doesn't need to break the entire rock at once.

It just keeps growing.

A little further.
A little deeper.
Until one day, there's a way through.

That's how I see learning.

I don't need to know everything today. I just need to understand the layer in front of me, work through it, and keep moving.

**One concept. One experiment. One failure. One breakthrough at a time.**
